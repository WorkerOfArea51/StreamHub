# StreamHub Release Notes 🎬✨

Welcome to the official release notes for **StreamHub** — the ultimate high-performance Android media streaming ecosystem.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.451 🚀

- ⚡ **Instant Video Resume & Zero-Delay Playback**:
  - **No More False Loading Wheels**: When you back out of a movie or episode and return to continue watching, playback now resumes instantly without flashing an unnecessary loading spinner when your video is already buffered.
  - **Faster Seeking & Scrubbing**: Jumping forward, rewinding, or resuming saved watch positions is now faster and smoother with zero frozen frames.

- ✨ **Design & Performance Refinements**:
  - Polished touch responsiveness, smoother transitions, and overall system stability improvements.
