# StreamHub Release Notes 🎬✨

Welcome to the official release notes for **StreamHub** — the ultimate high-performance Android media streaming ecosystem.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.456 🚀

- 🎮 **Brand New Cyber Capsule Buffering Display**:
  - Upgraded the buffering indicator to a sleek glassmorphic capsule anchored at the top of the screen, leaving the center of your video completely clear and unobstructed.
  - Features a smooth radar spinner, live buffer runway indicator, and real-time streaming speed.
- ⏱️ **Restored Video Scrubbing Preview**:
  - Horizontal swipe scrubbing now displays a dedicated center preview card with frame thumbnail, target time, and quick jump delta (e.g. `[+00:15]`).

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.455 🚀

- 🖐️ **Silky Smooth Screen Gestures for Brightness & Volume**:
  - Screen swipe gestures now stay in 100% real-time sync with hardware buttons and settings.
  - Eliminated initial jumps when starting a swipe — dragging now glides effortlessly from where the buttons left off.
  - Indicator sliders remain comfortably visible while your finger is on the screen and only fade out after you release.
  - Smoothed volume step transitions for perfectly natural sound level control.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.454 🚀

- 🎮 **Video Player HUD & Content Warning Restored**:
  - Restored Player Status HUD in screen corners (live clock, battery gauge, and remaining countdown).
  - Restored Content Advisory banner on playback start.
  - Restored network reconnecting countdown HUD and auto-dismissing resume prompts.
- 🎬 **Episode Runtime Badges & Watched Toggle Restored**:
  - Episode thumbnails now display their duration runtime badge and watch progress badge.
  - Long-pressing any episode card marks it as Watched / Unwatched.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.453 🚀

- 🎮 **Instant Player Controls Dismissal**:
  - Tapping anywhere on open video screen space now instantly dismisses the player controls and overlay, restoring seamless 1-tap show / 1-tap hide parity without waiting for the auto-hide timer.
  - Restored cinematic background dimming behind controls for clearer button readability.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.452 🚀

- ☁️ **High-Performance Hybrid Cloud Database**:

- 💎 **System Stability & Polishing**:
