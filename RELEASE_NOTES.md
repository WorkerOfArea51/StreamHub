# StreamHub Release Notes 🎬✨

Welcome to the official release notes for **StreamHub** — the ultimate high-performance Android media streaming ecosystem.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.441 🚀

- ⚡ **Deep System Polish & Resource Optimization**:
  - Fine-tuned screen rendering and navigation transitions for faster load times and an even lighter memory footprint.
  - Hardened application security rules and removed legacy network remnants for a streamlined, privacy-conscious streaming experience.
  - Polished background lifecycle monitoring, ensuring flawless handoffs when minimizing the app or listening in the background.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.440 🚀

- 🌐 **Intelligent Network Retry Engine**:
  - When saving shows, managing library entries, or synchronizing catalogs over weak Wi-Fi or mobile connections, StreamHub now automatically retries transient hiccups in the background with intelligent pacing.
  - No more manual retry prompts or interrupted catalog syncs during brief connection drops.
- 🔔 **Clutter-Free Personal Hub & Smart Broadcasts**:
  - Important community announcements and service updates are now delivered cleanly through native system notifications.
  - The Personal Hub stays clean, quiet, and personalized exclusively for your profile and viewing preferences, while owner broadcasting tools remain tucked away strictly for administrators.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.439 🚀

- 👤 **All-New Personal Hub & Profile Experience**:
  - **Hero Identity Card**: A warm, modern profile banner showcasing your custom avatar, display name, verified membership badge, and a convenient one-tap copy button for your unique Member ID.
  - **Grouped Preferences Container**: Redesigned settings and utility rows neatly arranged in unified charcoal cards with custom-crafted stroke icons for Watch History, Storage & Cache Management, Video Preferences, and About.
  - **Streamlined Visual Harmony**: Calibrated header heights across all five main tabs (Home, Explore, Downloads, My List, You) for pixel-perfect alignment.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.433 🚀

- 📑 **Reimagined Watchlist & My List Experience**:
  - **Asymmetric Hero Filter Dock**: Instant one-tap access to your saved collection organized into *All saved*, *Favorites*, *In progress*, *Watch later*, *Completed*, and custom *Folders*.
  - **Adaptive Media Filters**: Seamlessly filter your watchlist between Movies and TV & Anime with interactive buttons that adapt dynamically to your chosen app theme color.
  - **Tactile View Switcher**: Effortlessly switch between visual poster grid view and detailed playlist list view with satisfying haptic feedback.
  - **Playful Empty State Animation**: When your watchlist is empty, enjoy a smooth animated illustration with an orbiting accent dot and an expressive shortcut button to discover your next favorite title.
  - **Indefinite Download Retention ("Never")**: You can now choose "Never (Keep all)" to keep your downloaded episodes and movies permanently on your device without automated expiration.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.432 🚀

- 📥 **Cinema-Grade Offline Downloads Dashboard**:
  - **Live Storage Monitor**: Real-time dashboard card tracking your device's free disk space, total offline downloads, and active cache size with a smooth pulsing activity indicator and progress rail.
  - **Anchored Squircle Preference Menus**: Replaced bulky dialog popups with compact, anchored dropdown menus to effortlessly adjust download retention policies, toggle Wi-Fi-only downloading, or enable smart auto-resume.
  - **One-Tap Quick Actions**: Added an options menu with quick shortcuts to Storage Management and one-tap cleanup of completed downloads.

---

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.431 🚀

- 🔍 **Next-Generation Explore & Search**:
  - **Dynamic Search Header**: Expressive display typography with real-time active filter counters and an ultra-responsive search bar with instant clear capabilities.
  - **Fluid Category Switcher**: Smooth, liquid droplet animations when navigating between All Media, Anime, Movies, and Series.
  - **Rapid Discovery Chips**: Multi-select genre tags, quick-tap rating shortcuts (8.0+ Top Rated and 7.0+ High Quality), and recently searched terms with one-tap removal.
  - **Trending Highlights Showcase**: An eye-catching dynamic showcase highlighting top trending titles across three equal columns for balanced readability.
  - **Buttery-Smooth 120 FPS Feed**: Single-scroll catalog architecture that glides effortlessly across hundreds of titles without stutter or scroll friction.

### What's New in StreamHub v4.8.430 🚀

- 🏝️ **Floating Island Navigation Bar**:
  - Replaced traditional rectangular bottom bars with independent floating island chips that float cleanly above your content, giving more room to artwork and video cards.
  - Built-in tap shield prevents accidental touches on underlying posters when tapping between navigation chips.
  - Dynamic fluid viscosity animations that gracefully morph between tabs based on your chosen motion profile (Water Flow, Honey Viscous, or Snappy).
- 🎬 **Expressive Continue Watching Showcase**:
  - Redesigned card showcasing your active shows and movies with full-height previews, integrated progress bars, and rounded play controls.
  - Direction-aware card physics with swipe-to-reveal navigation and instant snackbar undo safety.
  - Tactile 90-degree snap rotation animation on the Home screen quick action button.
